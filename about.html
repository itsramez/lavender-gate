Our Mission

Safety, resilience, and opportunity for every community we serve

LavenderGate Association is a local civil society organization that designs and delivers inclusive, community-based programs aimed at enhancing the safety, resilience, and life opportunities of vulnerable populations. We primarily serve children, youth, women, refugees, persons with disabilities, and host communities, responding to both humanitarian and development needs.

We are committed to safe, ethical, and accountable programming, applying strong Safeguarding, Child Protection, PSEA, CHS, CPMS, and Accountability to Affected Populations (AAP) standards across all our interventions. Structured feedback, complaints, and referral mechanisms are embedded within our program design to ensure inclusion, protection, and continuous learning.
