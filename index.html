Who Are We

A community of people who believe in each other

LavenderGate is a local civil society organization serving children, youth, women, refugees, persons with disabilities, and host communities. We work hand-in-hand with the people we serve — from identifying needs to designing and delivering the programs that meet them.

What We Do

Focus areas that guide our work

From early learning to child protection, every program is designed around community priorities.

Education

Non-formal education, early learning, and remedial support for children.

Youth Empowerment

Empowerment, leadership, and employability pathways for young people.

Vocational Training

Competency-based skills training for youth and adults.

Psychosocial Support & Protection

Psychosocial support and child protection for children, youth, and caregivers.

Disability Inclusion

Ensuring equitable access and inclusion for persons with disabilities.

Health & Community Awareness

Health promotion and community awareness activities.
