What We Do

Programs built around real, everyday needs

Six focus areas, one goal: safety, resilience, and opportunity for the communities we serve.

01 · Education

Non-Formal Education & Early Learning

We provide non-formal education, early childhood development activities, and learning retention and remedial support — helping children catch up, stay engaged, and build the foundational skills they need to thrive.

02 · Youth Empowerment

Youth Empowerment & Employability

Our youth programs build life skills, leadership, and pathways to employment, preparing young people to participate fully in their communities and economies.

03 · Vocational Training

Vocational & Skills Training

Competency-based vocational training equips youth and adults with practical, market-relevant skills — building pathways to income and self-reliance.

04 · Psychosocial Support & Protection

Psychosocial Support & Child Protection

We provide psychosocial support for children, youth, and caregivers alongside dedicated child protection services, creating safe spaces for healing, resilience, and wellbeing.

05 · Disability Inclusion

Disability Inclusion

Our programs are designed for equitable access, ensuring persons with disabilities can fully participate in education, training, and community life.

06 · Health Promotion

Health Promotion & Community Awareness

We promote health and wellbeing through community outreach and awareness activities, helping families access the information and services that protect their wellbeing.

Our Approach

Community-led, every step of the way

We work in close partnership with communities, using participatory approaches that keep the people we serve actively involved — from identifying needs, to designing programs, to giving feedback once they're running. Services are delivered face-to-face in community centers, schools, and partner facilities, complemented where appropriate by accessible digital solutions that extend our reach into underserved and remote settings.

Through collaboration with local institutions, community actors, and national and international partners, we strengthen referral pathways, build local capacities, and support sustainable, locally led solutions — designed for equitable access, quality service delivery, and long-term impact.
